# 📖 Project Overview

QuickBite is an **end-to-end Power BI Analytics Dashboard** designed to analyze and monitor the performance of a food delivery business. The dashboard converts raw operational data into meaningful business insights, enabling stakeholders to make faster and more informed decisions.

The project focuses on four major business areas:

- 📊 Business Performance Monitoring
- 👥 Customer & Product Analytics
- 🚚 Delivery Operations Analysis
- 📍 Location Performance Insights

Using interactive visualizations, advanced DAX measures, field parameters, custom tooltips, and modern dashboard design principles, QuickBite provides a complete analytical solution for food delivery operations.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

Food delivery companies handle thousands of daily orders across multiple restaurants, customers, delivery partners, and locations. Without a centralized analytics solution, it becomes difficult to answer critical business questions such as:

- Which product categories generate the highest revenue?
- Which customer segments contribute the most orders?
- Which delivery locations experience operational delays?
- Why are customer orders being cancelled?
- How much revenue is at risk due to cancellations?
- Which operational KPIs require immediate attention?

The QuickBite Analytics Dashboard solves these challenges by transforming raw data into interactive dashboards that support strategic and operational decision-making.

# 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard monitors critical business metrics that help stakeholders evaluate operational performance, customer satisfaction, and revenue generation.

| KPI | Business Purpose |
|------|------------------|
| **Total Orders** | Tracks the total number of customer orders placed. |
| **Revenue** | Measures total revenue generated from completed orders. |
| **Revenue at Risk** | Identifies potential revenue lost due to cancelled orders. |
| **Average Customer Rating** | Evaluates overall customer satisfaction. |
| **Average Delivery Time** | Measures operational delivery efficiency. |
| **SLA Breach Rate %** | Shows the percentage of deliveries exceeding the defined SLA threshold. |
| **Orders Beyond SLA** | Counts deliveries completed beyond the expected delivery time. |
| **Average Minutes per KM** | Measures delivery efficiency relative to travel distance. |
| **Peak Hour Breach Rate %** | Evaluates SLA performance during peak business hours. |
